How it will work

  1. Agree the price in chat. Keep using WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or X as you do today.
  2. Vendor sends a safe-pay link. The buyer pays through it, and the money is held by a licensed payment partner — not sent straight to the vendor.
  3. Money is released on delivery. The buyer confirms the goods arrived as agreed, and the vendor gets paid. If something's wrong, the buyer can raise a dispute.
